# 🎓 RuralEdu - Digital Learning Platform for Rural Areas

## 📖 Overview

RuralEdu is an AI-powered Digital Learning Platform designed to provide quality education for students in rural and remote areas. The platform offers video lessons, study materials, quizzes, progress tracking, multilingual support, and parental monitoring to improve learning accessibility.

## 🚀 Features

### Student Module

* Student Registration & Login
* Personalized Dashboard
* Video-Based Learning
* Course Enrollment
* Online Quizzes & Assessments
* Progress Tracking
* AI Learning Assistant
* Multilingual Learning Support

### Parent Module

* Parent Login
* Student Progress Monitoring
* Attendance Tracking
* Performance Reports
* Notifications & Alerts

### Admin Module

* Student Management
* Course Management
* Content Upload (Videos & Notes)
* Assessment Management
* Analytics & Reports
